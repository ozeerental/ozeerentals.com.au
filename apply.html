RENTAL VEHICLE AGREEMENT TERMS AND CONDITIONS

This is an agreement between the prospective hirer identified above (you) and the car owner(s) to rent the motor vehicle described above including all accessories, tools, tyres, and equipment and any replacement vehicle (the vehicle).

1. VEHICLE CONDITION AND RETURN

The vehicle is delivered to you in good operating condition and reasonable order and with the seal on the odometer unbroken. You agree to return the vehicle in the same condition (except for ordinary wear and tear NOT INCLUDING WINDSCREEN AND TYRE DAMAGE) together with all tools, accessories, tyres, and equipment to the location specified and on the date specified (or sooner if demanded) to get a full refund of your bond money.

2. UNAUTHORISED AND PROHIBITED USE

Persons who must NOT drive the vehicle: (a) A person not identified in this agreement. (b) A person not licensed for that class of vehicle. (c) A person whose blood alcohol concentration exceeds the legal limit. (d) A person who provided false name, age, address or driver's license. (e) A person whose license has been cancelled or suspended within the last 3 years. (f) A person who has held an unconditional driver licence for less than 1 year. (g) A person under the age of 21. (h) Outside the area of use limitation. (i) On unsealed or off-road conditions unless authorised in writing.

GENERAL PROVISIONS

(a) You will promptly report any loss or damage to the vehicle. (b) All loss and damage per incident is subject to the damage liability charge. (c) We are not responsible if goods are left inside the car. (d) A late payment fee of 10% applies if invoice remains unpaid more than 2 days. (e) Bonds held for maximum 7 business days. (f) No bond refunded if you cannot abide by the agreement period.

3. FUEL, CLEANLINESS AND EXTERIORS

The vehicle must be returned with the same amount of fuel as at the time of rental. Same applies to the cleanliness and exteriors of the vehicle.

4. LIABILITY FOR PENALTIES AND ADDRESS UPDATES

During the hire period, the hirer is solely responsible for any penalties, fines, or misuse of the vehicle. The hirer must promptly notify Ozee Rentals of any changes to their address.

5. VEHICLE TRACKING

Most vehicles are equipped with GPS trackers. These allow monitoring of vehicle location and usage during the hire period.

6. VEHICLE INSURANCE COVERAGE

The Renter is liable for the applicable insurance excess in the event of any damage, loss, or incident. The authorized driver is solely responsible for their driving behavior and must ensure the vehicle is roadworthy before use.
